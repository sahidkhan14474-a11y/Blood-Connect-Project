KNOW THE FACTS

Don't believe every myth.

Correct information helps people make safe and confident decisions.

MYTH

Blood donation always makes you weak.

FACT

Many healthy eligible donors return to normal activities soon. Follow the staff's aftercare advice.

MYTH

Only people with a rare blood group can help.

FACT

Every blood group is important. Hospitals need different blood groups for different patients.

MYTH

Donation takes the whole day.

FACT

The full visit includes registration, screening, donation and rest. The centre can explain its timing.

MYTH

You can donate even when you are unwell.

FACT

Your health comes first. The medical team decides if donation is safe for you.
