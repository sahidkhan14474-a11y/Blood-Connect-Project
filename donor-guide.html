BEFORE YOU DONATE

A simple guide for blood donors.

Read basic information and always follow the advice of trained medical staff.

Things to remember

🥗

Eat properly

Have a healthy meal and drink enough water before donation, as advised by the centre.

🪪

Carry your ID

Carry the required identity document and share correct details during screening.

🩺

Health screening

Medical staff will check whether you are eligible to donate on that day.

After donation

  • Rest for the time suggested by the donation staff.
  • Drink fluids and have the refreshment provided.
  • Avoid heavy exercise for the period advised by staff.
  • If you feel unwell, inform the donation centre or seek medical help.

Important: Donor eligibility and aftercare advice can vary. Follow qualified medical professionals.
