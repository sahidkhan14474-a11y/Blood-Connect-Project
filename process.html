STEP BY STEP

How blood donation works.

Here are the common steps at a blood donation centre.

01

Registration

You provide your basic details and complete the donor form.

02

Health Check

Trained staff ask health questions and perform required checks.

03

Donation

If you are eligible, the donation is collected by trained professionals using sterile equipment.

04

Rest and Refreshment

Take a short rest and follow the aftercare instructions given by the centre.

Every step is important.

Never skip the health screening. Safe donation starts with informed decisions.
